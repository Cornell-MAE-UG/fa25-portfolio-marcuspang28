## Reflection

In Homework 3, I applied energy balances and heat conduction principles to derive governing equations and analyze temperature distributions in solids. One key part of the assignment was deriving the heat equation by performing an energy balance on a differential control volume, accounting for conduction, internal heat generation, and energy storage. This helped reinforce how the fundamental conservation of energy leads to the differential equations used in heat transfer analysis. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

The homework also involved solving steady-state conduction problems and using thermal resistance networks to determine heat flux and temperatures across materials with convection boundary conditions. Working through these problems improved my understanding of how engineers model real thermal systems and visualize temperature profiles across layered materials.
